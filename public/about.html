About us

SPQnC Consultant is a construction technical-services consultancy based in Pune, established in 2015. We work with developers, contractors and consultants in Pune and Mumbai.

Our work is the numbers behind a project: what it should cost, how much material it needs, and whether what is being billed matches what was built.

Who we work with

  • Developers who need budgets and tender documents they can rely on.
  • Contractors who need quantities, bar bending schedules and bills prepared correctly.
  • Consultants who need an extra pair of hands on measurement and checking.

How we keep our work accurate

  • We measure from your drawings and specifications, using standard methods of measurement.
  • Every assumption is written next to the item it affects, so you can check it.
  • Rates are shown with their source and date.
  • We resolve your queries before we issue the final document.
