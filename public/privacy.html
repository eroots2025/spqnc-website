Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form we receive your name, phone number, email address (if you give one), the service you need, and any project details you write.

Why we collect it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and to prepare a quote. We do not sell them.

Who handles it

Enquiries are delivered to us by Formspree, an online form service, which stores submissions for a limited period. Please do not put confidential drawings or bank details in the form. Send documents to us directly after we reply.
